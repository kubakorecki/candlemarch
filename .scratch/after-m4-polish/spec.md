# Polish after the M4 playtest

Status: open
Created: 2026-09-30
From: the owner's playtest notes (`.scratch/play/issues/after-m4.md`) and a follow-up list, with a screenshot of floating doors and windows on the woodcutter's hut and the Hut.

## Goal

Fix what the M4 playtest found in how the Valley looks and plays: villagers that pile up unseen, a bare start and row-like forests, repetitive trees, flat mountains, buildings with floating doors, too little control over woodcutting, goods dropped loose on the ground, and a Hero who is too strong and hard to find.

## Issues (dependency order)

01 Stockpile tiles hold 10 · 02 woodcutter's hut 1×1 · 03 Workplace yards · 04 building art on every turn · 05 tree tools in the woodcutter's hut · 06 trees at the start and organic forests · 07 tree species and variety · 08 mountains with relief · 09 villagers on one tile · 10 Hero vs Gloamlings · 11 select the Hero from the HUD

## Out of scope

Anything else in the playtest notes (food is done in `.scratch/food-balance/`).
