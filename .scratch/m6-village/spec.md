# M6: Village

Status: open
Created: 2026-09-25
Roadmap: `.scratch/v1/map.md`

## Goal

The Hamlet builds the Tavern and becomes a Village. Houses rise to the Craftsfolk tier, the late chains (flour, bread, tools, furniture) come online, masters arrive in every way the spec describes and bring their personalities, logistics gets its upgrades, and the crown starts collecting tribute.

Done when a player can go from Hamlet to Village, upgrade huts to cottages, and keep them supplied.

## Scope

From `docs/spec.md` (Progression; Villagers; Trades and masters; Logistics upgrades; Economy):

- Tavern: rank building (coin, planks, iron bars, ale in stock, 25 residents); serves ale; nearby villagers work a bit faster.
- Craftsfolk tier: cottage; needs two kinds of food, ale at a tavern, furniture.
- Village unlocks Miller and Baker invitations, cobblestone roads and handcarts.
- Mills (windmill on a hill, watermill on a river), bakery, smithy (tools), carpenter's workshop (furniture).
- Root cellar slows spoilage.
- Invitations: passing caravan, rescue, recommendation. Master traits.
- Crown tribute (leaning yes in the spec).
- From the M5 design sessions: reputation, Den bounties, the scrap smelter, deepening a mine, salvage and bricks for stone, bees and candles (tickets 18–23).

Roadmap correction: the smithy and carpenter's workshop are here (tools and furniture); the bloomery and brewery moved to M5.
