# Design session: Craftsfolk, invitations, traits and tribute

Status: ready-for-human
Type: grilling
Blocked by: m5-hamlet/21

## What

A `/grill-with-docs` session, held after the Hamlet playtest. Record answers in `docs/spec.md` and `CONTEXT.md`, then promote or rewrite the `needs-info` tickets that list this one.

## Questions

1. **Tier upgrade:** how a house's residents rise a tier (all needs met for how long?) and fall back; what the cottage costs.
2. **"Ale at a tavern":** do villagers visit the tavern (walk, drink), or is being in range enough? The tavern's work-speed radius and size.
3. **Furniture:** consumed once per house, or does it wear out?
4. **Mills:** what a "hill" is on the grid; watermill in winter (the river freezes).
5. **Caravan invitations:** visit schedule, stay length, how to win a master over.
6. **Rescue:** which masters wait in Ruins (M4 left the hook there); how the hero rescues them (with or without combat).
7. **Recommendation:** which masters recommend whom, and when.
8. **Traits:** the v1 pool (Drunkard, Proud, Early riser, Pious, …) and each effect in numbers; 1–2 per master, rolled with the PRNG.
9. **Crown tribute:** amount, due date, what penalties missing it brings, how "revoking privileges" works.
10. **Cobblestone roads and handcarts** under the chosen logistics model: cost, speed, capacity, who uses them.
11. **Bridges** (from the M4 design session, where Fords are the only river crossings): cost, length, whether Gloamlings cross them, and whether a Bridge can be closed at night.
12. **Renewing what runs out** (decided in principle in m5-hamlet/02): the scrap smelter (worn tools + charcoal → iron bars: rates, which master if any); deepening a mine (cost, how much ore, once per Deposit or per group); salvage (what share of a demolished building's stone returns); bricks for stone (which costs accept them, at what ratio).
13. **Reputation:** how it grows (coin traded, goods variety, Delivery orders) and what each level changes (merchant frequency, wanted-list size, import catalogue).
14. **Den bounties:** the second kind of side quest: reward, how a Den is named, time limit.
15. **Bees and candles:** the apiary (placement rules, honey and beeswax rates, season), the chandler (beeswax and tallow → candles; which master if any; where tallow comes from), and what wants candles (export only, or a Craftsfolk need).

## Done when

- Every question has an answer, and the dependent tickets are updated.
