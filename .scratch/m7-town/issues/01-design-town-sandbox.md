# Design session: Town, sandbox settings, heroes and the graveyard

Status: ready-for-human
Type: grilling
Blocked by: m6-village/16

## What

A `/grill-with-docs` session, held after the Village playtest. Record answers in `docs/spec.md` and `CONTEXT.md`, then promote or rewrite the `needs-info` tickets that list this one.

## Questions

1. **Guildhall:** exact cost; what "all tier-2 needs met" means for a 50-person village (every house Craftsfolk?).
2. **The win:** what the player sees on reaching Town; what endless play offers afterwards.
3. **Sandbox settings:** the levels of winter harshness and monster rate, and what each changes in numbers.
4. **Heroes:** the Ranger's and Smith-lord's bonuses in numbers (spec: Ranger forages/hunts faster nearby, slows game depletion, sees further at night; Smith-lord speeds bloomery and smithy nearby, tools last longer).
5. **Perks:** one per rank per hero — the list.
6. **Graveyard:** in or out of v1? If in: burial, and how unburied bodies affect happiness and disease risk (is disease in v1?).

## Done when

- Every question has an answer, and the dependent tickets are updated.
