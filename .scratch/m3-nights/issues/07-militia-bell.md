# Militia bell

Status: needs-info
Type: task
Blocked by: 01, 04

## What

- A bell (building or HUD button tied to one) calls villagers to arms: they drop their Jobs and fight. Ringing it again stands them down.
- Every fight costs production.

## Open questions (design session 01, Q6)

- Who answers, what happens to goods in hand, how the militia is positioned.

## Done when

- Headless tests: the bell pulls villagers off work, they fight, and they return to their Jobs.
- `pnpm check` passes.
