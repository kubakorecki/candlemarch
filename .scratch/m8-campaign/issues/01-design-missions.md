# Design session: mission framework and tutorial

Status: ready-for-human
Type: grilling
Blocked by: m7-town/08

## What

A `/grill-with-docs` session. Record answers in `docs/spec.md` and `CONTEXT.md`, then promote or rewrite the `needs-info` tickets that list this one.

## Questions

1. **Maps:** handcrafted mission maps, or fixed seeds of the generator?
2. **Objectives:** the shapes (reach rank, survive until, have master X, clear den Y, pay by date); optional objectives?
3. **Failure:** which conditions fail a mission (e.g. a key master dies); retry from the start or from a checkpoint?
4. **Tutorial:** how the game teaches (step prompts, highlighted UI, locked build menu that opens up)? Where the text lives (content).
5. **Story:** a narrator or characters between missions; how much text.
6. **Carry-over:** does anything carry from one mission to the next?
7. **Unlocks:** is the sandbox locked until the campaign is done?

## Done when

- Every question has an answer, and the dependent tickets are updated.
