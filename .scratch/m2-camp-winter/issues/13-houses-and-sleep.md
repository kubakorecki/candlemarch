# Houses, homes and sleeping at night

Status: needs-info
Type: task
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03

## What

- The hut gets a resident capacity. Villagers are assigned a home; the villager panel shows it.
- At dusk villagers go home and sleep; at dawn they return to work.
- Homeless villagers are visible (icon) and counted.

## Open questions (design session 01, Q5–Q6)

- Capacity, home assignment rule, what happens to tasks and goods in hand at dusk.
