# Design session: ranks, masters and charters

Status: ready-for-human
Type: grilling
Blocked by: none

## What

A `/grill-with-docs` session. Record answers in `docs/spec.md` and `CONTEXT.md`, then promote or rewrite the `needs-info` tickets that list this one.

## Questions

1. **Rank requirements:** how they're checked (residents, goods delivered to the rank building, coin); what exactly Hamlet unlocks in the build menu.
2. **What a master is:** a villager with a trade and personality, or a separate kind of person? Do masters need a better house?
3. **Master's workshop:** the speed bonus; how extra workshops of the trade are staffed by ordinary villagers; "slow to a crawl" without a master: how slow?
4. **Leaving:** why a master leaves (unhappiness only?) and what they take with them.
5. **Charters:** concrete conditions and signing fees for Carpenter, Mason, Butcher, Brewer, Smith.
6. **Notice board:** a building the player must build, or present from the start? Side quests in v1: which kinds?
7. **Master arrival:** where they arrive, and where they live.

## Done when

- Every question has an answer, and the dependent tickets are updated.
